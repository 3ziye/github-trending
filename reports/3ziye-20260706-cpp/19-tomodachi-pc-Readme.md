## 📖 How to Get Started
1. **Download** the latest pack using the button above.
2. **Pick your version** — 3DS or Switch.
3. **Follow the Setup Guide** — Clear instructions for your chosen emulator.
4. **Add your game files** — Use legally obtained ROMs.
5. **Import Miis** — Scan QR codes or load files and watch your island grow!
