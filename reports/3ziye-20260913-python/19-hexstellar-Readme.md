## Give your AI somewhere to send the hard part

Language models are extraordinary at understanding intent. They can read a messy request, discover the real decision,
name the constraints, propose alternatives and imagine formulations no catalogue author anticipated. But imagination is
not execution. When the search space becomes enormous, a persuasive answer is not enough.

**HexStellar gives the agent a computational counterpart.** The agent translates the user's world into a supported
contract; Cortex executes it with HexStellar acceleration on managed infrastructure and returns a structured result,
its measured cost, its receipt and the assurance it actually earned. The engine remains sealed. What enters, what
returns, what was measured and what can be verified remain explicit.

This is the manifesto:

> **Examples are recipes, not boundaries.** Do not search only for an example with the same industry name. Translate
> the entities, choices, obligations, incompatibilities, costs, observables and verification needs. Change the data,
> constraints, objective, scale and domain. Combine recipes when the contract permits it. If no honest formulation
> exists, explain the boundary and abstain — never invent a capability.

### One technology, two ways it reaches computation

| Surface | Where it runs | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| **Cortex** | HexStellar-managed infrastructure | Public CLI/API execution for supported decision, optimization, scientific-operation and verification contracts. Cortex requests use HexStellar acceleration and energy-efficiency technology on our servers; the user installs only the thin client. |
| **Enterprise Low-Energy Runtime & Acceleration** | Customer-controlled infrastructure | A licensed customer-deployed library for compatible compute paths in applications, frameworks, LLM stacks and other workloads. Access and evaluation are arranged directly with HexStellar under NDA. Public download and automatic activation are not available in 1.0. |

The distinction is deployment, not ambition: Cortex brings the problem to HexStellar's accelerated service; Enterprise
brings licensed acceleration to compatible computation in the customer's environment. Published energy and performance
figures stay attached to their named hardware, workload, denominator, correctness scope, date and evidence — never
silently generalized to every workload.

### It began with acceleration, not a problem catalogue

HexStellar did not begin with a list of optimization problems. While building **Trust Carbon Infrastructure**, another
startup he founded, **Brayon Pieske discovered a software foundation that accelerated compatible computation and reduced
energy use**. The next question was practical: what happens when that foundation is applied to mathematics, simulation,
structured decisions and verification? The experiments worked, grew into a computational platform, and became Cortex.

Cortex is how an AI sends a supported formulation to HexStellar's accelerated managed service. Enterprise Low-Energy
Runtime & Acceleration is how approved customers bring compatible execution into their own environment under licence and
NDA. The architecture is designed for qualified local, offline, edge and constrained-device deployments; exact IoT or
ESP-class support depends on an issued Enterprise build and is not a public-download promise in version 1.0.

```bash
pip install hexstellar
hexstellar demo
hexstellar route "describe the real problem you want my agent to formulate"
```

That is the starting point. The live catalogue teaches transferable shapes; `hexstellar capabilities` tells the agent what
is executable now; `validate`, `--dry-run` and `verify` keep creativity inside a measurable contract.
