## Troubleshooting

**FL25 ling?**
Increase ASIO buffer to 512 or 1024 samples and disable CPU energy-saving in Windows power settings.

**VST3 plugins not loading?**
Go to Options, Manage Plugins, and rescan your VST3 folder.

## System Requirements
- Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit), CPU with SSE4.1, 8 GB RAM minimum
